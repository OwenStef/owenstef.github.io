Critical Theory of Digital Lifeworlds

Habermas in the Age of Prediction

Senior thesis, Colorado College. Published in the Critical Theory & Social Justice Journal of Undergraduate Research (Occidental College), Vol. 14, May 2026.

JanFebMarAprMayJunJulAugSepOctNovDec
2026

The argument

Predictive algorithms filter and target what we read. I argue that this splits the public sphere into parallel segments, each with its own standard of what counts as credible, and those standards don’t fit together.

I use Habermas’s framework of lifeworld and system to explain why. Then I look at the EU’s Digital Services Act and Regulation (EU) 2024/900 as regulatory responses, and assess them.
